Methodology

Our 6-Step Growth Process

Marketing should work as a connected system, not isolated experiments. We follow a disciplined, transparent process that aligns strategy with tangible execution.

01

DISCOVER

We understand your business model, revenue targets, target audience profile, market landscape, existing assets, and current marketing friction points.

• Business & Unit Economics Audit
• Target Audience & Persona Definition
• Competitor & Category Landscape
• Existing Funnel & Asset Review
02

STRATEGIZE

We translate business objectives into a strategic marketing roadmap, defining key value propositions, high-converting offer structures, and optimal platform mix.

• Positioning & Differentiation Angle
• Core Offer & Lead Magnet Strategy
• Acquisition Channel Selection
• Budget Allocation & KPI Milestones
03

PLAN

We map out complete scopes, sprint deliverables, content calendars, tracking architectures, creative briefs, and execution priorities before launching.

• Editorial & Content Schedules
• Ad Creative Matrix & Copy Angles
• Tracking & Meta Pixel Architecture
• Campaign Timelines & Review Cadence
04

EXECUTE

We implement the campaigns: publishing high-quality creatives and reels, configuring paid ad sets, building landing pages, and setting up lead funnels.

• Ad Campaign Launch & Bidding
• Social Media & Reels Publishing
• Landing Page & Form Deployment
• WhatsApp Click-to-Chat Funnels
05

MEASURE

We monitor live data daily, auditing engagement, enquiry volume, cost-per-lead, click-through rates, and audience feedback with zero vanity metrics.

• Real-time Performance Tracking
• Cost Per Lead & Enquiry Quality Audit
• Creative Fatigue Identification
• Transparent Monthly Reporting
06

OPTIMIZE

We double down on top-performing creative angles, pause underperforming ad sets, refine messaging, and continuously improve the customer acquisition cost.

• A/B Creative & Headline Iteration
• Audience Expansion & Lookalikes
• Funnel Conversion Rate Enhancement
• Ongoing Scaling Strategy

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly do campaigns start running?

Following discovery and strategy approval, initial campaigns and creative assets are typically ready for launch within 7 to 10 business days.

Do you guarantee specific lead numbers or sales?

No honest agency can guarantee exact lead numbers or revenue, as performance depends on market dynamics, competition, budget, product quality, and platform algorithms. We focus on rigorous testing, data-driven optimization, and business-focused ROI.

Are ad budgets included in your agency fee?

No. Your advertising budget is paid directly to advertising platforms (Meta, Google, etc.). Our fee covers campaign strategy, management, creative development, and optimization.
