Meta Ads vs Google Ads: Which Is Better for Your Business?

By Nextvin Strategy Team Digital Marketing Insights

"Should we run Google Ads or Facebook and Instagram Ads?" is one of the first questions clients ask. The answer is not that one platform is superior — it is that they perform fundamentally different jobs in the customer journey.

The Core Difference: Intent vs Discovery

Google Ads captures existing intent. When someone types "emergency dental clinic near me" or "commercial interior designer Mumbai" into Google, they already know they have a problem and are actively evaluating providers. Google Ads places your business right at the moment of search.

Meta Ads generates new demand. Users do not scroll Instagram looking to hire an agency or buy a gym membership. Meta Ads interrupts their feed with compelling creative and irresistible offers, sparking interest in solutions they may not have been actively searching for that day.

When to Prioritize Google Search Ads

  • Urgent, high-intent needs (e.g. plumbing, repairs, specialized medical consultations)
  • Well-known categories where consumers actively search by keyword
  • B2B services with specific technical search terminology

When to Prioritize Meta Ads

  • Visually driven businesses (fitness, restaurants, salons, fashion, architecture)
  • New or innovative concepts that prospects don't yet know how to search for
  • Retargeting website visitors and keeping your brand top-of-mind

The Winning Strategy: Multi-Touch Synergy

The most effective marketing setups do not choose one over the other. They use Meta Ads to build broad local awareness and retarget website visitors, while maintaining Google Search campaigns to capture high-intent users at the exact moment of decision.
