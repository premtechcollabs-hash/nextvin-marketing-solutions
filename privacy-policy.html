Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Official Website: nextvinmarketingsolutions.in

Notice for Website Owner: This document serves as a foundational privacy policy template tailored for Nextvin Marketing Solutions. Please review all sections marked [REVIEW REQUIRED] to verify that data retention timelines and any third-party analytics tools reflect your active operations.

1. Overview & Scope

Nextvin Marketing Solutions ("Nextvin", "we", "our", or "us") operates the website https://nextvinmarketingsolutions.in/. We respect the privacy of our visitors and are committed to protecting personal data collected through your interactions with our website.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how it is used, how it is stored, and your rights concerning your personal information when enquiring about our marketing and growth services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal data voluntarily submitted by you when communicating with our team:

  • Direct Contact Enquiries: When you complete our Project Enquiry Form, we collect your Full Name, Business Name, Email Address, Phone Number / WhatsApp contact, Service of interest, Industry, estimated budget range, and project details.
  • Email Correspondence: Any communication sent directly to support@nextvinmarketingsolutions.in.
  • WhatsApp Communications: When you initiate a click-to-chat conversation via our WhatsApp link, your phone number and chat messages are received through the WhatsApp platform in accordance with WhatsApp’s privacy terms.

3. How We Use Your Information

Any data collected is used strictly for legitimate commercial and client-relationship purposes:

  • To evaluate your project requirements and prepare customized service proposals.
  • To respond to your questions and schedule strategy consultations.
  • To communicate during active client engagements and deliver marketing reporting.
  • To maintain internal business records and comply with legal requirements.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for advertising or unsolicited marketing.

4. Cookies, Analytics & Tracking

Our website is designed as a lightweight static site. We do not use intrusive third-party advertising cookies or trackers by default.

[REVIEW REQUIRED: Confirm if Google Analytics / Cloudflare Web Analytics is active] If website analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics or privacy-first Google Analytics) are enabled in the future, non-personally identifiable technical information (such as browser type, operating system, and general geographic location) may be processed to measure site traffic and performance.

5. Third-Party Services & Links

Our website contains links to third-party services, notably:

  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms): Click-to-chat links initiate a conversation in your WhatsApp app or browser. Your interaction on WhatsApp is governed by Meta’s privacy policy.
  • Hosting Infrastructure: The website is hosted via Cloudflare Pages, which maintains high security and content delivery network standards.

6. Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. Communications submitted via our website and email are stored securely.

We retain client and prospect enquiry details only for as long as necessary to fulfill the commercial purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with statutory accounting and tax regulations.

7. Your Data Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate information, or request the deletion of your enquiry data from our records.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@nextvinmarketingsolutions.in. We will respond promptly within standard business hours.
